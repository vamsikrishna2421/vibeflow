VibeFlow vs the market.

Most rivals make you pick one: offline or smart, Windows or polished, private or personalized. VibeFlow is the one doing all of it — a free, fully-offline Windows voice-to-text app that types anywhere, learns your words, and matches your own voice with a local AI. Here's where we lead, and where we're closing the gaps.

🔒 100% offline · nothing leaves your PC 🪟 Windows-native 🧠 Local-LLM personalization 🎯 Types anywhere · clipboard fallback 💸 Free

Where VibeFlow leads

  • Offline + Windows + local-LLM personalization in one — an essentially uncontested square
  • Voice-preserving AI formatting: keeps your first-person voice and exact words (rivals over-polish)
  • Teach-back vocabulary: learns the right spelling from your own corrections
  • On-device persona profiling: learns your domain & tone with no cloud
  • Zero-touch managed AI setup & non-technical polish (installer, tray, auto-update)

Where we're catching up

  • Per-app "Modes" — format by destination app in design
  • More recording modes: hands-free voice-activated start/stop, cancel hotkey
  • Text snippets / phrases & searchable dictation history
  • Voice editing commands ("scratch that", "select X")
  • Multi-language auto-switch; optional fast Parakeet engine
